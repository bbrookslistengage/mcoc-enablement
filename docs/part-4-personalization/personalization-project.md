## Overview

## Lesson overview

This section contains a general overview of topics that you will learn in this lesson.

- Planning a multi-section personalized campaign email for LEOptical
- Building the email structure using templates and content blocks from Part 3
- Pulling contact attributes from the data graph for personalized greetings and tier messaging
- Using Handlebars helpers for conditional content, loops, and formatting
- Querying Marketing Objects for product recommendations or tier thresholds
- Assembling all Parts 3 and 4 techniques into a single, complete email
- Testing the finished email against multiple contact profiles to verify every personalization path
