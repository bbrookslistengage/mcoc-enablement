# LEOptical — Client Profile

**LEOptical** is a direct-to-consumer eyecare and eyewear company offering comprehensive eye exams, prescription lenses, contact lenses, and vision health solutions. They run the **VisionCare Rewards** loyalty program with four tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) and sell four lens product families:

- **Visionaire UltraLux** — everyday precision lenses
- **Visionaire ChromaShift** — adaptive outdoor lenses
- **SeeClear DailyFocus** — smart everyday/screen lenses
- **SeeClear SunSync** — outdoor transition lenses

**Website:** https://leoptical.web.app/ (demonstration site)

**Their previous state:**
- Email marketing through a basic ESP (batch-and-blast newsletters)
- Loyalty program tracked in a separate system (exported as CSVs)
- Eye exam appointments managed through a clinic scheduling tool
- No unified view of customer across systems
- No consent management framework

**What they've hired us to do:** Build out their MCA implementation end-to-end.
